Privacy Notice

We only collect what you give us through the quote form — your name, contact details, vehicle, and project description — and we use it for one thing: preparing and sending your quote.

That information passes through Formspree (the form itself), and once we're in touch, may sit in Zoho (our email) or Stub.africa (our quoting and invoicing tool). We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone outside of running the business.

We don't hold on to it beyond what's needed. Once your quote has been sent, it's not kept on hand afterward.

Questions, or want your info removed sooner? Email katiso@lfd.ltd.

About LFD

loksheenracing

To build really cool race cars at a reasonable cost, and to raise the safety standard of South African motorsport.

LFD exists because of a love of racing that started young. Nothing fancy: my father, the garage and the car. What began as an expensive hobby turned into a moment of "wait, I can actually do this for a living."

LFD is a custom automotive fabrication workshop specialising in metal fabrication and performance development. We serve the car enthusiast who wants to get into racing or motorsport, the underdog nobody is rooting for.

We deliver value by fabricating parts that are readily available here, at a fraction of the price of buying them from overseas, so really cool race cars can be built at a reasonable cost. And by building to MSA standards and aiming higher, to FIA standards and beyond.

Safety first
Racing is dangerous enough. We build to MSA standards and aim higher, to FIA standards and beyond.
One standard
A small bracket and a full build are held to the same bar. If it isn't right, it doesn't leave the workshop.
Straight answers
