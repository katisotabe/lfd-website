Privacy Notice

We only collect what you give us through the quote form — your name, contact details, vehicle, and project description — and we use it for one thing: preparing and sending your quote.

That information passes through Formspree (the form itself), and once we're in touch, may sit in Zoho (our email) or Stub.africa (our quoting and invoicing tool). We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone outside of running the business.

We don't hold on to it beyond what's needed. Once your quote has been sent, it's not kept on hand afterward.

Questions, or want your info removed sooner? Email katiso@lfd.ltd.

About LFD

Built on
the work.

Loksheen Fabrication and Development started in a driveway in Brakpan, Gauteng — not as a business plan, but as a genuine obsession with doing things properly.

What began as weekend builds alongside my father grew into something more deliberate. A registered company. A set of standards. A belief that mechanical work done right is worth doing — and that the people building cars in this country deserve a workshop that takes the craft seriously. (No shade)

LFD is not a fast-turnaround shop. We take on limited work, give every build the full weight of our attention, and refuse to cut corners to hit a deadline. If that sounds like what you're looking for — we're probably the right fit.

We're based in Brakpan, Gauteng. We document the work. And we're only getting started.

Independent
No franchises, no shortcuts, no shareholders to answer to. Every decision is made in the interest of the build.
