About LFD

Built on
the work.

Loksheen Fabrication and Development started in a driveway in Brakpan, Gauteng — not as a business plan, but as a genuine obsession with doing things properly.

What began as weekend builds alongside family grew into something more deliberate. A registered company. A set of standards. A belief that mechanical work done right is worth doing — and that the people building cars in this country deserve a workshop that takes the craft seriously.

LFD is not a fast-turnaround shop. We take on limited work, give every build the full weight of our attention, and refuse to cut corners to hit a deadline. If that sounds like what you're looking for — we're probably the right fit.

We're based in Brakpan, Gauteng. We operate on weekends. We document the work. And we're only getting started.

Independent
No franchises, no shortcuts, no shareholders to answer to. Every decision is made in the interest of the build.
Hands-on
The work is done by the people who care about it most. Not farmed out, not rushed, not compromised.
Documented
Every build is recorded — on Instagram, on YouTube. The work speaks for itself and we're comfortable showing it.
Community-rooted
Based in Brakpan. Building here. The goal is to create dignified, skilled work in the community we come from.
